### Developmental Focus
Concrete observation, feelings language, map/sequence skills; no graphic content.

<div id="goals"></div>
### Learning Goals
- I can notice and describe what I see in historic and present‑day pictures of my city (observation).
- I can compare Then/Now and map where places are (comparison & geography).
- I can name helpers and actions that keep neighborhoods healthy (civic agency).

<div id="standards"></div>
### Standards Addressed (K-5)
- PA: 5.2 (Civic Rights & Responsibilities at elementary), 7.1–7.3 (Geography), 8.1 (Historical thinking), 4.2/4.3 (Watersheds & Environmental Health).
- NJ: 6.1 (U.S. History—civic life at elementary), 6.3 (Active Citizenship).
- DE: Civics 1 (Participation), History 2 (Historical Points of View), Geography 1 (Maps).

<div id="materials"></div>
### Core Materials
- Kid‑safe **news photography and aerials** only (distant exteriors).
- Large map of Philadelphia; sticky notes; sentence stems; crayons/markers.

<div id="sequence"></div>
### Lesson Sequence (5 x 45 minutes)
- Photo Talk & Feelings Chart — use 2 wide photos (see Source Sheet). Stems: “I notice… I wonder… This makes me feel… because…”
- Map It — place a sticky where each photo was taken; trace river/bridges; label ‘home/school/park’.
- Then/Now Draw — pair an older aerial with a recent wide exterior; students draw three changes.
- Helpers & Healthy Choices — brainstorm helpers (nurses, teachers, neighbors); sort actions: ‘kid‑doable’ vs. ‘grown‑up jobs’.
- Poster Share — create a Healthy Neighborhood poster with 1 photo, 3 labels, and 1 action pledge.
