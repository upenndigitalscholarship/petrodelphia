### Developmental Focus
Sourcing, CER writing, tradeoff thinking, structured oral testimony.

<div id="goals"></div>
### Learning Goals
- I can write a clear **Claim–Evidence–Reasoning** (CER) using data from air/health reports.
- I can analyze benefits/costs of industrial jobs vs. community health and safety.
- I can prepare and deliver respectful testimony for a public meeting.


<div id="standards"></div>
### Standards Addressed (6-8)
- PA: 5.1–5.4 Civics; 8.1–8.3 History; 4.2–4.8 Environmental & Ecology; 7.1–7.4 Geography; WHST.6–8 (disciplinary writing).
- NJ: 6.1 U.S. History; 6.3 Active Citizenship; Civics/Human Rights strands; writing in social studies.
- DE: Civics 1–4; History 1–3; Geography 1–3; ELA disciplinary writing.

<div id="lessons"></div>
### Core Lessons (6 x 50 minutes)
- Reading Mini‑Lab: Compare two air quality snapshots (e.g., 2019 vs. 2023) and highlight PM/benzene indicators (Philadelphia AMS).
- Photo‑CER: Select one news photograph (1970s) and connect to a data point in the Air Monitoring Network Plan.
- Tradeoff T‑Chart: Using Farmer (2025) + a PGW financial report, list ‘jobs & revenues’ vs. ‘risks & costs’.
- Remediation Literacy: Read an AOI Summary and identify contaminants, exposure pathways, and remedies.
- Testimony Workshop: Draft 2‑minute remarks to a hypothetical City hearing (tone, audience, ask).
- Hearing Day: Students deliver remarks; peers record 1 claim + 1 evidence for each speaker.
