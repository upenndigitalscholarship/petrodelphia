The Fatalities Map allows you to visualize the social and historical geography of fatal risk in the refinery zone. There are 84 green dots, each indicating the nearest intersection to the residence of an individual man who died in (or from) a workplace accident related to fossil fuel in South Philadelphia. There were scores more fatalities in this risk category, but not all can be verified to the level of name and residence. Click on each dot to see associated information about the accident type, location, and date, as well as information about the industrial worker or city firefighter who died. Although certain identifying details such as names have been removed, there is enough information for you to get a sense of individual lives and dreadful deaths. Be aware that this map is optimized for computer screens, not mobile screens. (Browse the genre tag "data sheets" for the underlying spreadsheet.)
